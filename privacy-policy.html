Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 18, 2026

Summary: LITHOS stores all data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.

Data Storage

LITHOS stores all your audiobook data locally on your device, including:

  • Library information (book titles, authors, cover art)
  • Listening progress and bookmarks
  • App preferences and settings
  • Playback statistics

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to back it up to cloud storage.

Cloud Backup (Optional)

LITHOS offers optional backup functionality to Google Drive and Dropbox. When you use these features:

  • You authenticate directly with Google or Dropbox
  • LITHOS does not have access to your cloud credentials
  • Backup files are stored in your personal cloud storage
  • We do not store copies of your data on our servers

You can revoke cloud access at any time through your Google or Dropbox account settings.

Analytics & Tracking

LITHOS does not include:

  • Analytics SDKs
  • Advertising frameworks
  • User tracking software
  • Behavioral monitoring

Your reading habits and app usage remain completely private.

Crash Reporting

If enabled, anonymous crash reports may be sent to help improve app stability. These reports contain:

  • Device model and Android version
  • App version
  • Technical error information

Crash reports do not include personal data, library contents, or listening history.

Third-Party Services

LITHOS may connect to the following services:

  • Open Library (openlibrary.org) - To fetch book metadata and cover art. Only book titles and author names are sent.
  • Google Drive / Dropbox - Only when you explicitly use backup/restore features.

Permissions

LITHOS requests the following permissions:

  • Storage - To access your audiobook files
  • Internet - For metadata lookup and optional cloud backup
  • Foreground Service - For background audio playback
  • Notifications - For playback controls

Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Export your library at any time via Settings
  • Delete all app data through Android Settings
  • Revoke cloud service permissions at any time
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local data

Children's Privacy

LITHOS does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app includes a Kids Mode feature with PIN protection that parents can use to restrict content.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of LITHOS after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
