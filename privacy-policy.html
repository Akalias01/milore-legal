Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Who is responsible for your data

MiLore is made by Mossglen Studio, California, United States. For anything in this policy — including any of the requests described under "Your rights" — write to support@mossglen.studio.

For the purposes of the GDPR, Mossglen Studio is the data controller for the small amount of usage data described below. We are not a controller for the audiobooks in your library or for anything you store in your own cloud account: we never receive that material and cannot access it.

Your data stays local by default

MiLore stores everything about your audiobooks — your library, your reading progress, your bookmarks, your settings, your goals, your finished books, and your badges — locally on your device. None of this is sent to Mossglen servers because we do not operate any servers that handle your data.

We do not have a backend. We never see your library.

Cloud sync (optional, user-owned)

You can optionally back up and sync your library across devices using your own Google Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive account. When you enable cloud sync, the app talks directly to those providers using their official APIs.

  • Your audiobook files, progress, and metadata go from your phone to your own cloud storage and back.
  • Nothing routes through Mossglen.
  • We never see your cloud credentials.
  • If you stop using MiLore tomorrow, your data stays in your cloud account where you can do whatever you want with it.

MiLore's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Why we are allowed to collect it, and for how long

Under the GDPR every use of personal data needs a lawful basis. Ours:

  • Crash reports and basic app activity — our legitimate interest in keeping MiLore working and fixing what breaks. You can object at any time by turning the setting off, and we act on that immediately rather than asking you to justify it. Where local law requires your consent instead, the setting is that consent, and withdrawing it costs you nothing.
  • Cloud sync and device-to-device transfer — performance of our agreement with you. These only run because you asked for them, and neither sends anything to us.

How long we keep it: usage and crash data is held by Google Analytics and Crashlytics on our behalf for a maximum of 14 months from collection, after which it is deleted automatically. We keep no separate copy. Aggregate figures that can no longer be traced to any single installation — for example a community badge percentage — may persist beyond that.

Where your data goes

Your library never leaves your device except to the cloud account you connect yourself, and that traffic goes straight to your provider.

The usage and crash data described above is processed by Google as our processor, which means it may be transferred to and stored in the United States. Google covers those transfers with the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and its certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. If you would rather no data left your device at all, turn the setting off in Settings → Advanced → Anonymous usage data — nothing is sent after that.

Account and email (future)

MiLore does not require an account today. In a future version we may offer optional accounts so you can sync across devices without a cloud provider, or to verify ownership for premium features.

If we add accounts:

  • You will be asked to verify an email address.
  • We will use the email only for account-related communication (sign-up confirmation, login verification, billing receipts if you have subscribed).
  • We will not send marketing emails or use your email for advertising.
  • You can delete your account and your email any time.

This is a future commitment, not a current practice. MiLore today does not collect any email address from any user.

Third-party services

MiLore connects to a small number of third-party services to enrich your library. Each is limited in scope:

  • Third-party book-metadata and cover-art providers — when you import a book, MiLore sends only the book title and author (or a book identifier) to look up cover art, narrator, series, and synopsis. We do not send any personal data.
  • Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive APIs — only used if you enable cloud sync, and only for your own account. Their privacy policies apply to the data you store in your account with them.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (Google) — receives the crash reports and basic app activity described above, if you have the toggle on. Subject to Google's privacy policy.

We do not use advertising SDKs, attribution SDKs, fingerprinting libraries, or any third-party trackers beyond the ones listed above.

Your rights

Most of your data never reaches us, so the fastest controls are the ones already in your hands:

  • Export your library any time using the Backup feature.
  • Delete all app data through Android Settings → Apps → MiLore → Storage → Clear Data.
  • Revoke cloud sync permissions through your Google, Dropbox, or Microsoft account at any time.
  • Turn off usage statistics through Settings → Advanced → Anonymous usage data.
  • Uninstall MiLore at any time; nothing remains on Mossglen servers because nothing was ever sent there.

For the usage data we do collect, the law also gives you rights you can exercise against us directly. You have the right to ask for a copy of that data, to have it corrected, to have it erased, to restrict or object to how we use it, to receive it in a portable form, and — where we rely on your consent — to withdraw that consent at any time without giving a reason. Write to support@mossglen.studio and we will answer within one month. There is no charge.

One honest limitation: the data is tied to a random per-install identifier and nothing else, so to act on a request we may need you to tell us that identifier from the device in question. If you have already uninstalled the app or cleared its data, we have no way to find your records, and no way to confirm they were ever yours.

If you think we have handled your data badly, please tell us first — but you do not have to. If you are in the EU, the UK, or another country with a data protection authority, you have the right to complain to it directly, and you can do that instead of or as well as contacting us.

Children's privacy

MiLore is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

In the European Economic Area and the UK, the age at which someone can agree to this kind of data use on their own is 16 in most countries, though some have set it lower — as low as 13. If you are under that age where you live, please ask a parent or guardian to read this with you and decide together, or simply turn off usage data in Settings → Advanced. Nothing in MiLore stops working when you do.

If you believe we have collected data from a child below the applicable age, contact us at support@mossglen.studio and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top, post the new version at mossglen.studio/privacy, and mirror it in the in-app Privacy Policy screen. We will not weaken protections without giving you advance notice through the app.
